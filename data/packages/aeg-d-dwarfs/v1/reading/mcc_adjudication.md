## Sheet A (structure, distance, magnitude)

1. m_032 / 2002AJ....124..310C / structure: s1 is right. Text p.8 prints `r_eff,R = 32″` and Table 1 prints `32.0`. These are different digits, so they get two rows, and s2 merged them. Both readers missed two values in the Fig. 5 caption on p.30: φ′_inner = −20° (different digits from −20.0) and ǫ_inner = 0.15. Both were added.
2. ngc_6822 / 2007ApJ...655..863D / magnitude: s1 is right. Table 2 (p.30) prints a V flux density of 2.24 Jy. It gets its own row with unit Jy, not mag. s2 put it in a note only.
3. sagittarius_1 / 1998ApJ...508L..55M / magnitude: s1 is right. The abstract (p.2) prints M_V as the range −13.4 to −14.6, which gets its own row. s2 put it in a note only.
4. sagittarius_1 / 2003ApJ...599.1082M / structure: s1 is right on the five values s2 lacks. These are the centre (284, −30)° on p.7 and three quoted values on p.9: PA 104.8 ± 1.2, PA 108.4 and ǫ 0.80 ± 0.15. Both readers missed two values: ǫ = 0.62 − 0.65 (range, p.9) and the quoted 3:1 axis ratio (p.13). Both were added. Locations were also added where the same digits are printed again: 0.65 on p.6 and p.9, 0.62 on p.9, 104.3 on p.9.
5. sagittarius_dirr / 2000AJ....119..777L / magnitude: s1 is right. The colour–colour value E(B−V) = 0.00 ± 0.04 (p.3) gets its own row, and s2 put it in a note only. Both readers missed E(B−V) = 0.0 in the captions of Fig. 4 (p.12) and Fig. 6 (p.13), which is different digits from 0.00. It was added. Locations were also added for 0.06 (abstract, Fig. 6) and 0.125 (Fig. 4).
6. tucana_1 / 2009ApJ...699.1742B / distance: s2 is right. The lower panel of Fig. 20 (p.18, page image) prints "Tucana (m−M)=24.88", and s1 missed it. A p.5 location was added to 24.74.
7. ugc_04879 / 2011A&A...527A..58B / magnitude: s2 is right. Table 1 (p.6, page image) prints 8.2^{+1.6}_{−1.4} × 10^6 L_V⊙. The value is written as 8.2e6, so the errors are 1.4e6 and 1.6e6. s1 wrote 1.4 and 1.6.
