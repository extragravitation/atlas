# First-naming search log: pre-arXiv systems (D-049a item 5)

Searched 2026-10-30 by the main session on NASA ADS (ui.adsabs.harvard.edu), with scans read in SciX Scan Explorer through JvH's browser. ADS does not index full text for these scans, so each search was by title and year, sorted by date ascending. Candidates were read in date order until one named the system a galaxy (in any wording listed in item 5).

| System | Query | Candidates (date order) | Chosen | Grounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sculptor, Fornax | title:(Sculptor Fornax), years 1938–1945 | Shapley 1938 BHarO 908 (Sculptor discovery: "a transition type of stellar system", X); Shapley 1938 Nature (Fornax discovery, abstract: "a stellar system of a new kind", X); **Baade & Hubble 1939 PASP 51, 40**; Shapley 1939 PNAS 25, 565 | Baade & Hubble 1939 | L: luminosity and dimensions compared with "neighboring extragalactic nebulae"; "evidently dwarf, extragalactic nebulae" |
| LMC, SMC | title:(Magellanic), years 1890–1930 (84 records; titles screened) | Pickering 1897–1904 and Leavitt 1905–1912 (variable stars; no kind stated in titles); Shapley 1922 BHarO 775 (read: the LMC is "the star cloud itself"); Shapley 1924 HarCi 255 (read, p. 1: no kind named); Bernheimer 1924, Lundmark 1924, Shapley 1925 BHarO 816 "Comparison of Messier 33 and the LMC" (not read); **Hubble 1926 ApJ 64, 321** | Hubble 1926 | L: classification "based on the forms of the photographic images"; the Clouds included among the extragalactic nebulae |
| IC 1613 | title:1613, years 1900–1965 | Wolf 1906 (discovery; no scan available); **Baade 1929 AN 234, 407** | Baade 1929 | L (borderline): "a star cloud of the type of the Magellanic Clouds" |

**Limits.**
- For the Magellanic Clouds, three 1924–1925 papers were found but not read (Bernheimer, Lundmark, Shapley's comparison with M33). One of them may name the Clouds as galaxies before Hubble 1926. Any such paper predates every kinematic measurement of the Clouds, so its grounds cannot be dynamical. The chosen paper stands as the earliest found by this search, not as proven earliest.
- IC 1613's discovery note (Wolf 1906) has no scan.
- All these readings were made by the main session, which also runs the conversion. Under D-049a item 6, a second reader (JvH) checks a sample: Sculptor (Shapley 1938), Fornax and Sculptor (Baade & Hubble 1939) and IC 1613 (Baade 1929).
