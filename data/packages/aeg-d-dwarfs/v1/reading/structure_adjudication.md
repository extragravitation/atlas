## Conventions applied

- Quoted values: literature values a paper prints, either as properties of the system or for comparison, are recorded, marked "quoted from …" or "quoted from … (comparison)". Where both readers missed such values in a pair under review, the adjudicator added them (source `adjudicator`).
- Single-star values (individual RR Lyrae, individual eclipsing binaries) are left out and named in the pair note. Where a system value rests on one star but is printed as the system result (Hydra II §4, Grus II Table 5), the system printing is kept and the per-star table row is left out.
- Also left out as not values of the system: a control field (Boötes III CF E(B−V)), a different system on the line of sight (Grus II field Chenab/Orphan RR Lyrae), the Sagittarius stream distance (Belokurov+2007), and fits to simulated data (Cas III §4.3.2). Sub-population and component values the paper prints (Fornax sub-populations, Eri II old/intermediate luminosities, Aqu 2 BHB MV) are kept and labelled.
- One row per printed value: a repeat with the same value digits is one row listing every location, even if the repeat omits the error or adds "∼"/"≈". A printing whose value digits or printed error digits differ is its own row (e.g. 8.69 ± 0.75 vs 8.7 ± 0.8; 154.0 vs 154). Distinct estimates whose digits happen to coincide (different fits, fields, fiducials or calibrations) stay as separate rows.
- Approximate signs are kept in `value` as printed, normalised to the printed glyph: "∼" (U+223C), "≈", "≃". Qualifier words ("about", "almost") go in `note`, not `value`. Upper limits are written "<x"; ranges as printed ("19.7 to 19.9", "[0.01,0.03]"). Powers of ten are written "× 10^n" as printed.
- Coordinates are kept as the base reader wrote them, with separators and the unit (hms/dms/deg). Coordinate errors printed in arcsec or seconds go in the error columns with the printed unit symbol.
- Errors: statistical errors go in the error columns; a systematic error goes in `note`, unless the paper gives only a combined error. Applied to Crater II (Vivas+2020: 116.5 kpc, stat ± 0.2 in columns) and Eridanus II (Martínez-Vázquez+2021 Table 5: σrand 0.01 in columns, σsys in note). An error not labelled systematic (e.g. σ⟨µ0⟩) stays in the columns.
- Errors are recorded as printed, even when malformed: Segue 1 "0.31 ± 013" keeps "013" (decimal point missing in the print).
- Prose rows (method, band_note, profile): the clearer reader's row is taken; every distance `method` row was checked against the paper.
- `source`: `both` = both readers have this row with identical numbers; `r1`/`r2` = only that reader; `adjudicator` = added or changed by the adjudicator (including normalisation that matches neither reader). `checked_page` lists the pages named in `location`, all of which were checked.
