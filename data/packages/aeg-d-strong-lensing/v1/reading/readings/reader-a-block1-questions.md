# Reader A, block 1 (SLACS): questions and open points

Date 2026-10-31. Records read: R5, R7, R13 (2009 paper and 2008 letter), R49 (Grillo et al. 2010; Eichner et al. 2012), R66. `reading.csv` holds 1497 rows. It was generated by `build_reading.py` from the files in `dl/`, which are hashed in `hashes.txt`. Values are copied as strings. The only operation applied to them is the z_l < 0.30 selection.

## Specific questions

1. **R61 (is R_ap the Einstein radius?)** Not in my scopes (block 1 is SLACS). Not read.
2. **R57 (aperture of the SuGOHI III stellar mass)** Not in my scopes. Not read.
3. **R26 and R29 (light-profile fit)** Not in my scopes. Not read.
4. **Gas candidates (inventory §4) in my scopes.**
   - **R49, Eichner et al. 2012 (J1430+4105).** §4.2, second paragraph: "this gas needs to be incorporated in either the dark matter or the de Vaucouleurs" component. The paragraph goes on to cite Young et al. 2011 for M(H2) ≲ 10⁹ M⊙, which the paper calls "less than 1%" of its masses, and Sanderson et al. 2003 for hot gas "up to 10%". It concludes "the uncertainty of our mass estimates due to the neglected gas is ≈ 10%". This is a literature statement about the kind of system. Nothing was measured for this lens. (arXiv:1209.2140v1)
   - **R5, Bolton et al. 2008: a statement not listed in §4.** §5.2, paragraph "It is worth noting…": the coincidence of mass and light peaks "requires that the dark-matter component and any significant gas mass be well aligned" with the stellar spheroid. It is a sample-level statement about alignment and gives no amount. It is recorded once as system `(sample)`.
   - R37 (Ferreras et al. 2008) is a SLACS alternative, but it is not one of the records assigned to me, so I did not read it. R8 and R64 are X records.
   - I found no system-level gas statement in R7, R13 (either paper), Grillo et al. 2010 or R66. R66 discusses dust in the centre of the lens, not gas (§2.2, Appendix A). Each low-z system has a `gas = none` row.

## Points the rules did not settle

1. **Overlap of scopes.** J1538+5817, J1430+4105 and J0946+1006 are all in R5 Table 5 and in R13. Their membership is now checked: all three are R5 grade-A SIE models, and all three are in R13 and R7. They therefore appear twice: in "SLACS V-modelled lenses" with the R13 default, and in their own scopes with the defaults named there (R49, R13, R66). The rows are complete in both places, so the comparison script can key on scope.
2. **Counts.** R5 Table 5 lists 63 V-modelled systems. 54 of them have printed z_l (zFG) < 0.30. Each single-system scope has one system, and all three are below 0.30.
3. **Kind.** Six low-z V-modelled systems are not coded early-type by R5. J0841+3824, J0955+0101, J1032+5322, J1251−0208 and J1432+6317 are coded L. J1103+5322 is coded U, and R7 gives it "S0/SA". R7 gives "S" for the five L systems. None of the six is in R13 2009. I recorded kind from both R5 (Mph code) and R7 (Morph) for all 63.
4. **θ_E errors.** R5 prints no per-system error on b_SIE. §6.1 adopts "a 2% RMS fractional error" as a sample statement. This is recorded in the note, and err cells are blank. R13 assumed "a conservative 5% error" on the same θEin for its Mtotlen.
5. **R13 aperture.** For all 48 low-z systems in R13 2009, its θEin (VizieR `thetaE`) equals the R5 b_SIE as printed (a string comparison, nothing computed). The inside-R_E masses come from the de Vaucouleurs aperture factor f_ap, with θe printed per system. The masses are printed as integers in units of 10¹⁰ M⊙ with asymmetric errors.
6. **R13 2008 letter.** This is a different sample of 15 lenses. It uses Salpeter + BC03 only, and its REin comes from Treu et al. 2006 / Koopmans et al. 2006 (SLACS II/III), not R5. I recorded it as record R13 with the note "2008 letter". Two of its lenses (J0216−0813, J0737+3216) are above z = 0.30. J1330−0148 is not among R5's 63 models, so it has no row.
7. **R7.** M_E is printed as log10 with no error, and r_Ein in kpc (quantity `aperture`). R7 does not state that the old lenses re-use R5's models. Its stellar masses are totals with a σ–metallicity prior (alternative only, D-063 item 3). J0109+1500 and J0405−0455 have "..." for stellar mass (fewer than 2 HST bands).
8. **J1538+5817 (R49 default).** The paper prints only a total, 20 (+1, −4) × 10¹⁰ M⊙, with a single IMF (Salpeter) and a de Vaucouleurs profile. Its θe = 1.58″ is taken from Bolton et al. 2008 (Table 1 cites R5); it is not a new fit. Whether the profile from R5 counts as "the source's own fit" (§3.1) is for the rules to decide.
9. **J0946+1006 (R66 default).** The stellar masses (Table 8: per component and total, Chabrier and Salpeter) are fitted to the double-tPIEMD photometry (Table 3), not to the double Sérsic (Table 2). R66 §5: the log masses change by 0.06 with the Sérsic description. The frozen `sersic_projected_fraction` cannot evaluate tPIEMD components. I recorded both profiles. tPIEMD r_c and r_t are entered as `R_e` rows with "NOT an effective radius" in the note, because the brief has no quantity for them. The component "share" is printed only as an F814W magnitude. The outer-ring z_s2 of this system is photometric, but R5's zBG (0.6085) is the spectroscopic first source.
10. **J1430+4105 (R49 alternative).** Eichner Table 9 prints 6.7 / 4.4 / 4.0 × 10¹¹ M⊙ (A Salpeter; B, C Chabrier), with no errors and no explicit "total". The paper models a nearby galaxy group (GI) as part of the lens, which may matter for the kind rule (group lens).
11. **Flatness.** No record says "flat" in words. All of them print Ωm = 0.3 and ΩΛ = 0.7, and that is what the `flat` rows record.
12. **Routes and versions.** R5, R13 and R7 VizieR tables were read from vizier.cfa.harvard.edu (`asu-tsv`). vizier.idia.ac.za was refused by the proxy (403). R7, Grillo 2010 and R66 tables come from content.cld.iop.org ascii files, fetched with the default curl user-agent and no spoofing (version `MRT`). Text statements come from the arXiv PDFs: 0805.1931v1, 0911.2471v1, 0904.3282v2, 0712.0680v1, 0912.0744v1, 1209.2140v1 and 1111.4215v3. The R7 values from IOP agree with VizieR J/ApJ/705/1099 (216 of 216 strings). The R5 b_SIE values agree with the arXiv Table 5 (54 of 54).
