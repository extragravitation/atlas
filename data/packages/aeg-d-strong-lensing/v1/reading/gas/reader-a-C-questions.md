# Part G: open questions (reader A, 2026-11-01)

1. **Extra `quantity` values.** I used two values the brief does not list: `none_printed` (full text read, but it prints no gas mass, ratio or limit in any radius; 7 rows: C2, C3, C5, C6, C7, C9, C10) and `NS` (no full text reached; 6 rows). Should these be renamed?
2. **Single-dish CO beams used as radii.** I recorded C15, C17 and C21 (and C16's 22.5″ square for its limits) as `gas_mass_in_radius`, with the printed beam FWHM (a diameter) as the radius. Two papers say the beam misses gas: C14 (§3.4) says only 7 CARMA galaxies sit "well contained" in the 21.6″ beam, and C25 (§2.2) says the central pointing holds a median 57% of the CO. Is a beam diameter an acceptable stated radius? And should the 10 kpc test use radius = half the FWHM?
3. **Papers that call single-pointing masses "Total".** C18 (no beam size printed) and C19 (summed over pointings) print masses with no radius. I described these by table rather than writing per-galaxy rows. Should they get per-galaxy rows?
4. **C21 column ambiguity.** The Table 4 header says M(H2), but the text says "The derived Mgas values, displayed in Table 4" with Mgas = 1.36 M(H2). It is not settled whether the printed values include helium. C21 is also biased: cooling-flow BCGs at z 0.016–0.234, where a 22″ beam may exceed 10 kpc. I did no conversion.
5. **C17 brightest quarter.** I used the 48 rows of Table 3, which include 5 galaxies not observed (shown as '–'). This gave 12 galaxies by MB, 9 of them with values. Should the quarter come from the 43 observed galaxies instead?
6. **C13 and C30 brightest quarter.** Neither paper prints MK or M* per galaxy (both point to ATLAS3D Paper I), so I could not select per-galaxy rows from them. The catalogue_location rows name the tables. The only printed high-mass statistics are C13's 6/25 detected for MK < −24.4 (M* > 10^11 at M*/LK = 0.82, as printed) and 1/13 for MK < −25.
7. **C4.** The gas/stellar ratio (< 0.04%, NGC 4486 ~0.4%) has no stated integration radius, so I recorded it as `gas_total_no_radius`. JvH may want to check the profile range in Fig. 1 or in Churazov et al. 2010.
8. **C8.** The two rows within 10 kpc (NGC 4459 and NGC 4526, both S0) come from the arXiv v1 submitted version. The MNRAS version was not checked and its values may differ.
9. **C20** is a two-page meeting abstract, not refereed. I recorded it for completeness. Under the refereed condition in §3 it may need to be dropped.
10. **NS (C11, C12, C22, C23, C24, C31).** None has an arXiv record. A VizieR keyword search (vizier.cfa.harvard.edu, `-words=`) returned no catalogue, and two queries timed out. I did not try content.cld.iop.org, because building an article URL needs a DOI or path I do not have from a source. A DOI from ADS or the citing papers' reference lists would let a reader retry.
11. **Versions.** Everything was read from arXiv (versions in the CSV). No journal or VizieR machine-readable tables were fetched for C13, C15, C17 or C30. Values were transcribed from pdftotext output, and ⊙ symbols are sometimes lost in the quotes.
