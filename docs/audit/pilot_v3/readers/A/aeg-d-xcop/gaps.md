# Method gaps: aeg-d-xcop/v3 pilot audit (reader A)

Neither row needed any arithmetic. g_obs = GOBS and g_bar = GBAR are copied unchanged, and no constants were used. The gaps below are steps I had to infer, plus places where the written method or the locators do not match the files.

1. **HDU not stated in METHOD.md.** The method says "a cluster's FITS table" but does not name the HDU. I used HDU 1 (the only BinTable; HDU 0 is an empty primary). The locator also says hdu 1, so the two agree. I inferred this from the file.
2. **Output precision and representation not stated.** The columns are FITS `E` (float32). The method does not say whether to report the float32 value (e.g. 2.2402964e-10) or its float64 expansion (2.2402964205969766e-10), or how to round. I report the shortest float32 form in values.csv and the float64 expansion in the notes.
3. **Error convention file missing.** §2 says to read GOBS_ERR_LO/HI and GBAR_ERR "as error magnitudes (`reading/error_form.md`)", but that file is not in the packet. I followed the sentence in METHOD.md. I could not check the referenced file. The errors do not affect g_obs or g_bar.
4. **Unit string equivalence.** The file's TUNIT is `m/s^2`. The method says "m s⁻²". I treated the two as the same unit. The method does not say what counts as passing the unit check.
5. **"The four accelerations" is wrong in number.** The file has five acceleration columns (GBAR, GBAR_ERR, GOBS, GOBS_ERR_LO, GOBS_ERR_HI). I checked all five; all are m/s^2.
6. **Mapping from measurement_id to file and row is not in METHOD.md.** I took it from rows.json `source_ref`. The method does not say how the IDs are ordered. Assuming the order of the files listed in §1 (A644 19 rows, A1795 21, A2029 22, A2142 20, A2319 27), m000078 = 19+21+22+16 = A2142 row 16. This is consistent with rows.json, but it is inferred. `tables/measurements.ecsv` is not in the packet, so I could not check it.
7. **Locator section wrong for l. 263.** rows.json and METHOD.md §4 cite "§3.4, l. 263". In hydromass.tex, §3.4 (Nonparametric log-normal mixture reconstruction) starts at l. 226. \subsection{Optimization} (§3.5) starts at l. 256, so l. 263 is in §3.5. The quoted content ("we evaluate it at the radii of the observed data") is on l. 263 as stated. The line is right; the section is not.
8. **BCG dynamical mass-to-light ratio claim not at the cited line.** The GBAR source_ref says the source's g_bar includes "the central galaxy's dynamical mass-to-light ratio" and cites l. 456. Line 456 is only the g_bar equation. The dynamical M/L statement is in §2.4.1, l. 103–107 (l. 107: "We use this best fitting stellar mass-to-light ratio to derive the enclosed stellar mass of the BCG"), and no locator points there.
9. **Path mismatch in sources.json.** S3 `file` is `eprints/ep_2205.01110v1`, but the packet has it at `files/ep_2205.01110v1`. It is a gzip tar that must be unpacked to reach hydromass.tex, and the method does not say so. Its sha256 matches sources.json.
10. **Line numbering convention not stated.** I assumed that "l. N" means physical line N of hydromass.tex, counted from 1 (`sed -n Np`). Lines 263, 410, 453 and 456 match the quoted content under that convention.

Verified: the archive sha256 and all 20 member sha256 values match sources.json. The e-print sha256 matches. Both FITS files have TUNIT kpc for RADIUS and m/s^2 for the acceleration columns.
