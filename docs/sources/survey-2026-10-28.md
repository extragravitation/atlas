# Source survey for the taxonomy (2026-10-28)

Three surveys run on 2026-10-28 to fill the coverage matrix in `docs/plans/TAXONOMY.md`. Each was read at source in that session, to the depth stated; "unverified" means it could not be confirmed. **None of these sources has been fetched, hashed or converted, and none is ruled.** Licences are mostly unread: confirm each before any data are redistributed. AI-assisted (Claude), checked against the URLs given where marked.

### Strong lensing
SLACS IX, Auger et al. 2009, ApJ 705, 1099 (arXiv:0911.2471): mass inside the Einstein radius; stellar masses from population fits (Chabrier and Salpeter); VizieR J/ApJ/705/1099; no rights statement; 85 grade A + 13 grade B; IMF and 2D-vs-3D choices; obtainable.

### Weak lensing beyond KiDS
Mistele et al. (arXiv:2310.15248): KiDS DR4 lensing to g_bar ≈ 10⁻¹⁴·⁹ m s⁻²; release of lensing products not stated. HSC, DES: no g_obs–g_bar release found.

### Satellites and special configurations
Seo, Sohn & Lee (arXiv:2010.00693): stacked satellite dispersions, not profiles; weak fit. Júlio et al. 2025, A&A 704, A330 (CC BY): Jeans-derived acceleration profiles for 12 dwarfs (dwarf cell). Tidal dwarfs (Lelli et al. 2015, A&A 584, A113): 6, tables unverified; equilibrium caveat. Polar rings (Lüghausen et al. 2013, arXiv:1304.4931): data across ~8 papers. NGC 1052-DF2: KCWI dispersion 8.4 ± 2.1 km s⁻¹ (arXiv:1901.03711); distance 13 Mpc (Trujillo et al. 2019) vs 22.1 ± 1.2 Mpc (Shen et al. 2021); author table at https://www.pietervandokkum.com/ngc1052-df2 . NGC 1052-DF4 (van Dokkum et al. 2019, ApJL 874, L5): 7 globular-cluster velocities.
