# Screening S9, reader A: stellar masses of galaxy clusters

Reader A (AI, Claude), 2026-10-31. Records S1 to S19 as listed for this round. Brief: `docs/sources/screening-brief-S9.md`.

How the sources were read: each arXiv abstract page (`https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>`) and each arXiv LaTeX source (`https://arxiv.org/e-print/<id>`). From the source, a script pulled out only the section headings, sentences from the data, observation, photometry, membership and stellar-mass method sections, table and figure captions, table column headings, and the cluster-name and redshift columns. Numbers were masked except counts, redshifts and apertures. VizieR ReadMe and directory pages were read where the host allowed it. No data tables were downloaded. "Unverified" means the item could not be read at source. Journal references marked "jref unverified" were matched to their arXiv preprint by title, authors and the search result only, because the arXiv page carries no journal reference. No stellar masses, fractions, M/L values or results are recorded here.

Abbreviations: SPS = stellar population synthesis; BC03 = Bruzual & Charlot 2003; stat. = statistical background subtraction; spec = spectroscopic; photo-z = photometric redshift; tier as in the brief.

## Questions for the maintainer

- **Q1. Dynamical M/L.** S3a, S3b, S5 and S18b convert light to mass with a single M/L from dynamical modelling (Cappellari 2006 SAURON, or a value corrected for dark matter), not with an SPS model and named IMF. S12a uses a dynamical (Jeans) M/L for the BCG part. Should these count as eligible, or fall under the FP/FJ-type exclusion? S3a, S3b and S18b are the only records here that measure the ICL directly in local clusters.
- **Q2. IMF not named in the paper itself.** S1a inherits Salpeter and Padova from Fritz+11 (S1c). S4a and S4d use Kauffmann+03 M/L with no IMF stated. S9b uses FSPS with no IMF stated. Is a named IMF in a cited method paper acceptable?
- **Q3. Per-cluster values only in figures, or not released.** S7a (136 XXL) and S14b (260 DES-ACT) computed per-cluster stellar masses but print neither a per-cluster table nor cluster names (S14b: "on reasonable request"). Accept at tier 3, or exclude?
- **Q4. Galaxy-level member catalogues.** S1b, S1c and S19 give stellar masses for individual member galaxies but no cluster totals or profiles. S1b covers A1644, A3158 and A3266; S19 covers A2255. Are member catalogues that would need our own summation eligible, given the rule against deriving numbers ourselves?
- **Q5. Stellar mass functions only.** S13b (Perseus, within about 0.25 r200) and S13f (9 MACH clusters including A2255, within R200 and 3R200) give per-cluster SMFs but no totals. Is integrating a published SMF acceptable?
- **Q6.** S14c gives ICL fractions and mass-profile slopes but no absolute masses. Exclude as "fractions only"?
- **Q7.** S13e (A1689 core): the sections read do not state membership for the mass map. Accept as a core-only BCG plus bright-galaxy map?
- **Q8.** S9b's μ* is called a mass proxy and uses a richness-scaled aperture. Treat as a measured stellar mass (with Q2 still applying)?

**Target-cluster coverage.**
- **A1644, A3158, A3266**: stellar-mass coverage only in S1a (A3158 and A3266; P) and the galaxy-level S1b and S1c (P).
- **A2255**: stellar-mass coverage in S4b and S4c (E), S5 (P), S13f (P) and S19 (P).
- **RXC J1825**: no stellar mass in any record. Both X-COP records list it without one.
- **X-COP**: S12b's table confirms that A1644, RXC1825, A3158, A2255 and A3266 have no X-COP stellar mass.

**Blinding note.** Several arXiv abstracts, shown in full on the abstract pages, contain qualitative or numerical results. Some were seen while reading. None are recorded here. The extraction script masked decimals and percentages in the method text, but some unmasked numbers inside table rows reached my screen. In LMS 2003 (S2b) these included integer K-band M/L entries (Υ500) for A1651, A2255 and A3266. In Laganá 2011 they included r500 and gas masses. None of these numbers are recorded here. S2b is excluded on method grounds that do not depend on those values. VizieR ReadMe files were readable only for J/ApJ/816/98, J/MNRAS/493/4591 and J/A+A/555/A66; the others are marked unverified.
