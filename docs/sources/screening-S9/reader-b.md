# S9 full-text screening, reader B (entries S1 to S19)

Screened 2026-10-31 against `docs/sources/screening-brief-S9.md`. One row per record. Where a paper and its own VizieR catalogue are both listed, they share one row and the row says so.

How the sources were read: arXiv abstract pages and PDFs (text extracted locally; only the abstract, data, photometry, membership and stellar-population sections, table captions and column headings, and cluster-name and redshift columns were inspected). VizieR content was read only where a page could be reached: a cdsarc ReadMe, a GAVO VO-registry page, or a cdsarc directory listing. ADS, aanda.org and most cdsarc ReadMe URLs could not be reached from this session.

Blinding disclosure: during extraction, numerical rows from a few tables appeared on screen before they could be masked: Lin+2003 Table 1, Andreon 2010 Table 1, Cariddi+2018 Table A.2 (one row), and Eckert+2022 Table 4 (one row). The VO summary of Hsu+2022 also showed a multiple-core fraction, which is not a stellar quantity. None of these values is recorded anywhere in this file.

Abbreviations: SPS = stellar population synthesis; BC03 = Bruzual & Charlot 2003; spec-z / photo-z = spectroscopic / photometric redshift membership; stat-bkg = statistical background subtraction.

## Questions for the maintainer

1. **Dynamical or empirical M/L.** S2b, S3a, S3b and S5, the BCG part of S12a, and probably S18b convert light to mass with an M/L taken from dynamical modelling (Cappellari+2006 SAURON Schwarzschild models, JAM, or "dynamical analysis of ellipticals") rather than SPS with a named IMF. Does this count as "a named IMF and population model", or is it excluded in the spirit of the Fundamental Plane / Faber–Jackson rule? A dynamics-based M/L would also feed dynamical information into g_bar.
2. **Apertures from scaling relations.** Many records measure stellar mass inside an r500 or r200 that itself comes from an M–T, L_X–M, SZ, σ, weak-lensing or HOD relation (S4b, S7a, S9b, S10a, S10b, S18c and others). The brief excludes masses "normalised by M500". Is an aperture defined this way acceptable, provided the stellar mass is a direct sum?
3. **Galaxy-level catalogues and per-cluster SMFs.** S1b, S1c, S13b, S13f and S19 give member-galaxy stellar masses or per-cluster SMFs but no cluster total. May the Atlas build cluster totals or profiles from them (with completeness corrections), or should they be treated as inputs only?
4. **Unverified identities.** That S1b's bibcode is Pérez-Millán+2023 is inferred, not read. S13d (2021ApJ...915..106C) could not be identified; please supply the title or DOI.
5. **WINGS IMF.** WINGS-SPE II (S1c) names a Salpeter IMF for its models but reports masses "normalized to the Kroupa (2001) IMF" in its comparisons. Please confirm which IMF the released catalogue, and hence S1a's profiles, uses.
