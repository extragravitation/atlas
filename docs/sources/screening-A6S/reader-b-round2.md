## Questions for the maintainer

1. **Spectroscopic stellar-population M/L (R61, R62).** The brief asks for stellar mass "from photometry". R61 pairs a photometric luminosity with a reference Kroupa M/L from spectral fitting (Newman et al. 2017), and R62 gives a spectral-fitting M*/L with a free IMF. Does a spectroscopically fitted, non-lensing, non-dynamical M/L count as a baryonic input? If yes, R61 becomes E (pair Newman et al. 2017). If no, R61 becomes E-pair (observed side).
2. **Einstein radius from image separation without a fitted model (R69e, and the SIS step in R69a).** Is "half the image separation" or a circle fit to arc peaks acceptable as the observed side? The brief requires a lens model fitted to the lensed images.
3. **R68 identity.** No 2024 Lemon paper on CASTLES was found. Candidates read: Lemon et al. 2024 Space Sci. Rev. review (arXiv:2310.13466, no CASTLES content) and Lemon et al. 2023 MNRAS 520, 3305 (Gaia quasar-lens confirmations). Please supply the bibcode or DOI. The CASTLES website itself has no licence and no lens models.
4. **R60 and R69 group environments.** Should a single-galaxy-dominated lens in a group (R60, Cosmic Horseshoe) stay in A6, with the environment recorded as a property, or be routed to A8? The same question applies to the R69 systems flagged as group-scale.
5. **Overlap R63/R67.** All 23 R67 lenses are re-modelled in R63. Which model should be the primary observed side under the duplicate rule?
