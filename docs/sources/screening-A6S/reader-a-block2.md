## Questions for the maintainer

1. R32 (and similar cases): is an Einstein-aperture luminosity times a stellar-population M/L taken from an assumed age/metallicity range (not fitted per galaxy) an acceptable §1a baryonic input, or must the M/L come from a per-object SED or spectral fit?
2. R33: a stellar M/L from full-spectrum (MUSE) stellar-population fitting is independent of lensing and dynamics but is not "from photometry". Does §1a admit spectroscopic stellar-population masses?
3. R38, R44, R58: candidates with lens models but unknown or assumed source redshifts. Is the observed side (Einstein mass) eligible only when z_s is measured, and does a neural-network-predicted SIE (R58) count as a lens model fitted to the images?
4. R41 (2016): when the IMF is a fitted parameter of the SPS model, should the atlas take the stellar mass at a fixed named IMF (if tabulated), or treat the record as photometry-only?
5. R47 (SDSS J0100+1818): the authors call the deflector a galaxy/group, and a later paper calls it a "lens group". Should it go in A6 or A8?
6. "Photometry only" records (R35, R36, R45, R46, R52–R55): should these be coded E-pair (observed side plus photometry), or should a missing stellar-population M/L step count as a P with a named pairing? I coded E-pair throughout for consistency.
7. R44 / R58: candidate grades (A/B) vary by survey. Is there a minimum grade, or a confirmation requirement, for eligibility?
