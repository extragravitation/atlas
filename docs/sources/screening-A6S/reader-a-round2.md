## Questions for the maintainer

1. **R68**: please give the full citation or bibcode for "Lemon et al. 2024 (CASTLES)" from R59. No such paper was found. The only Lemon-related resource reached (the Cambridge lensed-quasar list) cites neither CASTLES nor a 2024 paper.
2. **R60**: the Cosmic Horseshoe lens lies in a group, although the authors attribute almost all of the deflection to the single galaxy. Does it go in A6 (galaxy-scale deflector) or A8 (group)?
3. **R63 vs R67**: R63 re-models the SLACS lenses with the same pipeline and a larger sample. Should R67 be treated as superseded (X, duplicate) or kept as an independent observed-side measurement?
4. **R62**: does a full-spectrum SSP-fit stellar M/L (free IMF, central ~1″ only, no photometric SED) qualify as a baryonic input under §1? Its aperture does not match R_Ein.
5. **R69a and R69c**: Negrello et al. 2014 (arXiv:1311.5898) supplies the stellar masses that R69c uses. It is not in R1–R69 and should be added for screening. The IMF and SPS model of the R69a stellar masses were not stated in the text read.
6. **R69e**: is a θ_E taken as half the image separation, measured directly from the images rather than from a fitted model, acceptable as the observed side? It is coded X here because full models exist in R69d and R69f for most systems. Two systems (J085358.9+015537 and J141351.9−000026) appear in neither of those.
7. **Not verified anywhere this round**: GitHub (R63 repository and licence) and the VizieR/GAVO ReadMe pages could not be reached from this session (proxy refusal).
