## Questions for the maintainer

1. **Spectroscopic SSP M*/L as the baryonic side (R31/R33, R32, R41-2016).** Is a stellar M/L from full-spectrum SSP fitting (with a named IMF and SSP library), applied to imaging luminosity, acceptable as the "luminosity plus stated stellar-population M/L" input? If yes, R31 + R33 pair for ESO286-G022 and R32 stays E. If no, R32 becomes E-pair (observed side plus photometry).
2. **Stellar fraction instead of stellar mass (R34 XLENS I).** Table 4 gives the SPS stellar mass inside R_E only as a fraction of the lensing mass (from Dye et al. 2008). Accept fraction × published lens mass, or require refitting the SDSS colours?
3. **Assumed source redshifts (R38 Faure 2011; R44).** Should systems whose source redshift is assumed or unknown be admitted with a flag, or held until spectroscopic z_s exists?
4. **IMF of the COSMOS stellar masses (R38).** The Bundy et al. 2006 code's IMF is not stated in the section read. This needs checking before admission.
5. **Restricted Zenodo record (R45).** The files are not accessible and the paper could not be located. Hold until it is public?
6. **Kind (R47, R48, R35 MG2016+112, R49 Tu 2009, R53).** Several deflectors sit in or are described as groups. The galaxy-vs-group criterion needs ruling (e.g. one dominant galaxy within R_E, versus multiple lens components modelled).
7. **Candidates with fitted models (R44).** These are not "candidates only" in the protocol sense (they have SIE models), but they are unconfirmed and lack z_s. Confirm that P/lead is the intended treatment.
