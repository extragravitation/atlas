## Questions for the maintainer

1. **Q1 (L22b and L22a):** Kluge 2021 Table 2 prints the members-only integrated satellite brightness (g′, image field of view, background-subtracted, no membership or completeness correction). L22a Table 4 prints BCG+ICL. Does adding two printed columns from companion papers count as "printed" under D-071, given that the apertures differ (field of view versus profile integration)? If not, both are X.
2. **Q2 (L24a Coma, L24b A2390):** These papers print L_ICL (and L_BCG+ICL) together with ICL fractions of the total cluster light (BCG+members+ICL, within a stated projected radius), but not the total itself. Is dividing one printed value by another "rebuilt by the reader"? It is not rebuilt from galaxy catalogues. A ruling decides P→E or P→X.
3. **Q3 (LV4, LV5a, LV7a):** Several catalogues print per-cluster summed luminosities with (a) FoF membership and no aperture radius, or (b) a magnitude-truncated sum with background subtraction but no faint-end completeness correction. Do these meet D-071's "membership and completeness corrections … within a stated aperture", or are they X?
4. **Q4 (CDS access):** The CDS ReadMe pages (cdsarc, viz-bin, GAVO column lists) could not be reached from this session for LV1–LV6 and LV7b, so they are coded NS. LV3 (Proctor 2015) is the strongest candidate on the paper text and should be confirmed from its ReadMe first. Can the maintainer allow cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr for a follow-up pass?
5. **Q5 (L23b):** Gonzalez 2000 (A1651) is coded E, but an erratum (ApJ 611, 1208, 2004) exists and was not read. Should it be read before the record is accepted, or only at extraction?
6. **Q6 (L28a/b):** These are old Chinese Astronomy and Astrophysics papers with no arXiv version, and ADS was blocked here. The L28b abstract mentions a B-band total luminosity for Virgo. Is a library scan worth requesting?
