## Questions for the maintainer

1. **BCG not stated (L2, L4, L8).** Each luminosity covers "all galaxies" (L2 summed in an aperture; L8 all spectroscopic members) or is an analytic Schechter integral (L4), and none names the BCG. Does D-071 accept implicit inclusion, or must the BCG be named at source? A Schechter-only total, as in L4, arguably replaces the BCG with the fitted form.
2. **Band not named in the column (L5c).** Table A1 col. 9 gives L_op without a band; the text uses the i band. Confirm from the CDS ReadMe (J/A+A/461/397), which I could not fetch.
3. **Printed components, no printed total (L10).** Red and blue members and the BCG are printed separately within the same r500 aperture. Is a sum of printed components eligible, or does it count as a reader rebuild?
4. **Faint-end treatment varies.** L1 stops at a fixed faint absolute-magnitude limit. L2, L4 and L5a extrapolate or correct. L8 multiplies by a fixed factor. L9 applies no correction. Should D-071 require a statement or a common convention?
5. **Projection.** L1 and L4 are deprojected to 3D. L2, L5a, L5c, L8, L9 and L10 are projected. L5b deprojects but prints no per-cluster values. Confirm that both are acceptable if labelled.
6. **Possible follow-ups outside this batch (not screened):** the Giuricin et al. 2000 NOG group catalogue (behind L3b); the Ursa Major companion to L12 (Kopylova & Kopylov 2009, arXiv 0912.2503), which may resemble the paywalled L12 papers; the Lin, Mohr & Stanford 2003 paper I (astro-ph/0304033).
