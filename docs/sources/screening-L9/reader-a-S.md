## Questions for the maintainer

1. **Magnitudes as luminosities.** S3a prints absolute magnitudes (I band, with extinction and k corrections in a separate column, not applied). S10a prints observed-frame apparent 3.6 µm magnitudes. Do these count as "printed luminosity" under D-071, or as reader-rebuilt? I scored S3a E and S10a P.
2. **Model-fit luminosity.** S1a prints only a Sérsic-fit effective (half-light) luminosity per cluster. The integrated profile itself is in figures only. Is a fit parameter eligible, given there is no luminosity within a stated r500/r200 aperture?
3. **Unstated BCG inclusion.** S4b, S4d and S6 sum or integrate member light but never say whether the BCG is inside the total. S16b implies it. Accept implicit inclusion, or ask the authors?
4. **Corrections that change the quantity.** S16b is evolution-corrected to z = 0. S6 is magnitude-limited (K ≤ K*+2.5) with no faint-end extrapolation. Should these be eligible as printed, or carried with a flag?
5. **Mixed provenance inside one table.** S4d's A2667 value is a literature K-band luminosity, filter-transformed and extrapolated to r500. Exclude that cluster only?
6. **Duplicates.** S3a and S3b share the same imaging for 12 clusters (S3b supersedes). S18c's local set is S2b. A1651, A2589, A2255 and A1367 recur across S2b, S3, S4b and S4c. A deduplication rule is needed before ingest.
7. **Unconfirmed identities.** S13d (2021ApJ...915..106C) is matched only tentatively, to Contini & Gu (arXiv:2104.05913). S13f's volume and page are not on arXiv. Please confirm bibcodes from ADS.
8. **Aperture from lensing (S6).** r500,WL comes from weak lensing. Does the Atlas allow a lensing-set aperture on a dynamics-channel luminosity, or should it be flagged as cross-channel?
