## Questions for the maintainer

- **Q1 (BCG in Table 2):** Text (§II, §IV.1) says total light includes the BCG, but I did not see a Table 2 note confirming that L500/L200 are the "with BCG" values. Accept the text statement, or require a table-level confirmation (e.g. journal version) before E?
- **Q2 (3D aperture):** The luminosities come from projected counts converted to a sphere with an assumed galaxy concentration (c_g = 3), not a direct deprojection of the data. Does a model-based cylinder-to-sphere correction count as "3D" under D-071, or should it be recorded as projected-with-model-correction?
- **Q3 (reproducibility):** The background-count relation is cited as a private communication. Does an unpublished background model affect eligibility, or only the provenance note?
- **Q4 (radius source):** r500 is set from an M–T_X scaling relation, not hydrostatic or lensing masses. Flag only, or does the atlas need to note that the aperture depends on a scaling relation?
- **Q5 (BCG unstated; fixed apertures; L2, L4, L5a, L5c):** These print per-cluster luminosities, but the BCG is not explicitly addressed (L2, L4, L5c), and L2/L4/L5a use fixed metric apertures (L2 also R_vir from a companion paper). Is "sum of all galaxies" enough for BCG inclusion, and are fixed metric apertures acceptable under D-071?
- **Q6 (L3 mapping):** I matched 569..101 to astro-ph/0109134 and 569...91 to astro-ph/0109132 by title only; the arXiv versions print "ApJ, submitted". Both are X either way.
- **Q7 (L5a blinding incident, CDS):** While reading astro-ph/0403354, the mediating fetch volunteered a fitted scaling-relation slope and scatter. These are not per-cluster luminosities and are not recorded, but you should know I saw them. Separately, CDS ReadMe pages (cdsarc) were blocked, so the electronic-table columns for L5a/L5c are unverified.
- **Q8 (BCG implied, L8, L9):** L9 implies the BCG is in the total (BCG as fraction of total; magnitude window starts at the BCG) but never says so; L8 never mentions it. Accept implication, or require a statement or author contact?
- **Q9 (L10 columns):** L10 prints red-member, blue-member and BCG luminosities in separate columns within r500. Summing printed columns: allowed, or "rebuilt by the reader" (X)?
- **Q10 (NS):** L7b, L12a, L12b, L13a and L13b could not be read in full (no arXiv; publisher/ADS pages blocked or paywalled). They need library access.
