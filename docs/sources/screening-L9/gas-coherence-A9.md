# Gas coverage and programme coherence of the pairable A9 clusters

- **Date:** 2026-10-31. Prepared by Claude (AI assistance, disclosed). Exploratory screening record, not a data package and not a choice of sample. Scripts, extracted name lists and download hashes are in `gas-coherence-tools/`.
- **Names only.** This file records cluster names, redshifts as printed, record identifiers, decision codes, centres, aperture definitions, radial ranges and method names. It records no mass, density, luminosity, temperature, fraction, ratio or other result value. Positions were used only inside the matching scripts; they are in the tools folder as extracted (name and position columns only) and are not reproduced here.
- **Scope.** The 79 clusters of `coverage-A9-v3.md` whose stars pair with an observed side under the ruled (recommended) QL2 column: 55 pairable with lensing only, 23 with member dynamics only, 1 with both (Coma). They were regenerated by re-running `coverage-v3-tools/cov3.py` and the pairing logic of `coverage-v3-tools/gen.py` (copied into `gas-coherence-tools/pairable.py`); the counts reproduce v3 (55 / 23 / 1).
- **Rules applied.** `docs/methods/A9_PRECEDENCE.md` (D-073): §1 item 4 (gas: X-ray or SZ density in radial bins, or the source's fitted density model within its measured range), §3.1 (gas order: X-COP, CHEX-MATE, the gas source named by the observed side's survey, others; binned before parametric), §1 item 6 (projected stars with row L, 3D stars with row O).
- **Not readable or not read:** CDS/VizieR, NED and HEASARC refused the connection from this workspace (proxy 403), so no name resolver was used; positions come only from the papers' own tables. Not read: Nagarajan et al. 2018 (APEX-SZ, named by C18d), Akamatsu et al. 2011 (A2142, named by C28a per the A9 reading), the XXL-100-GC cluster list (Pacaud et al. 2016), the CHEX-MATE project database. See "Gaps".

## Method

1. **Clusters.** The 79 pairable clusters, with their A9 records, observed-side channels, and counting star records with geometry, exactly as coverage v3 defines them (recommended QL2, v2 main rule).
2. **Gas candidates.** For each cluster, every gas source that covers it, in §3.1 order:
   - **X-COP** by name (12 clusters of the Atlas package).
   - **CHEX-MATE** by a PSZ2 name form already carried by v2, else by position: any paper position for the cluster (Mulroy 2019, Martino 2014, CHeCS, CLASH-X, Rines 2013, Pizzardo 2021, CAIRNS, Umetsu 2020, Hicks 2006, Mulroy 2014, L5a) within 3′ of the CHEX-MATE X-ray peak **and** |Δz| ≤ 0.02. The tolerance was set before the match was run. All 17 positional matches fell within 1.7′ (most within 0.3′).
   - **The gas source named by the observed side's survey**, found from the A9 readings and the papers' own text: CLASH → CLASH-X (C3a); LoCuSS → Martino et al. 2014 and Mulroy et al. 2019 (both name the LoCuSS X-ray analysis; Okabe & Smith 2016 cites Martino et al. 2014 among the LoCuSS papers); HeCS → CHeCS (C20c); XXL → Eckert et al. 2016 (XXL Paper XIII, cited by C18c); APEX-SZ → Nagarajan et al. 2018 (named by C18d; not read); Coma (C23c) → Mirakhor & Walker 2020; A2142 (C28a) → Akamatsu et al. 2011 (per reader B; not read) and X-COP; CHEX-MATE kinematics (C29b) → CHEX-MATE. CIRS (C20d) and CAIRNS (C21a) name no gas source.
   - **A9 gas-side E-pair records and E records with their own gas**: C3a, C9h/C9i, C15a, C9g/C9h, C11a, C11b, C20c, C29a, as listed per cluster in v2/v3.
   Membership was by normalised name (the v3 rules) or position, against lists read at source. Martino's "ABELL 0115s" was matched to A115 by position (0.01′ from Mulroy 2019's "Abell0115"; flagged). MS 1455.0+2232 = ZwCl 1454.8+2233 was confirmed by position (0.03′).
3. **Status of each gas source under §1 item 4**, from the texts read:
   - **A**: per-cluster binned density, or fitted density-model parameters, tabulated or released: X-COP (public release, packaged), Peng 2009, Hicks 2006 (β-model fits), Okabe 2014, Chappuis 2025, Li 2024 (parameters, Table 2), Umetsu 2009 (SZ model fit only).
   - **B**: profile in figures only (tier 3), or a fitted model whose per-cluster parameters were not seen: CLASH-X (inputs on MAST), CHeCS, Mirakhor & Walker 2020, Eckert 2016.
   - **C**: aperture gas masses only, not a profile or model: Martino 2014 (r2500, r1000, r500, hydrostatic radii), Mulroy 2019 (within r500,WL). These do not meet §1 item 4 as written.
   - **D**: not published or not read: CHEX-MATE density profiles, Nagarajan 2018, Akamatsu 2011.
4. **Coherence** (per cluster and channel). For every combination of one observed-side record in the channel, one counting star record of matching geometry, and one gas candidate, count how many of the three come from one programme or team (3, 2 or 1). The table reports the highest-scoring combination; ties are broken by gas status (A > B > C > D), then by a shared centre, then by a shared aperture. This is a description of what exists, not a choice. Programmes: LoCuSS (C6a, C6c/C6d, L8, S6, Martino 2014, Mulroy 2019); CLASH (C1a–C1c, C4a, C5a, C3a); XXL (C18c, L9, Eckert 2016); the SAO caustic team (Rines, Geller, Diaferio: CAIRNS C21, CIRS+HeCS C20d, HeCS C20a, CHeCS C20c, Coma C23a/b); X-COP (package, S12b); CHEX-MATE (C29b, C9g/C9h, the sample); others as named in the table. Lin & Mohr (L1, S2b) is a separate team from the SAO caustic team.
5. **Centres.** Each centre was taken from the source text found by searching for centre statements. "Not stated in text searched" means none was found; "not read" means the paper was not opened here. "Shared" is yes only when all three are stated and are the same kind (BCG; X-ray peak; X-ray centroid; galaxy-distribution centre). An X-ray peak and an X-ray centroid count as different.
6. **Apertures.** The star aperture is the source's (D-071 safeguard 3: one radius). Gas: profile or model (read at the star aperture), or aperture-only. "Same aperture" is yes only when stars and gas are given inside the same defined radius.
7. **Scripts.** Python 3 with `-I`, run from the session scratch folder, with downloads kept in a separate folder. `run.sh` reproduces `gas.json` and the table from the PDF texts, byte for byte (checked).

**What the evidence says, stated once:**
- In row L, two programmes are internally coherent: LoCuSS (42) and XXL (12). Neither publishes per-cluster gas profiles in a form that meets §1 item 4: LoCuSS gives aperture gas masses only, and XXL gives profiles in a figure only.

- In row O, no programme supplies all three. The best case is X-COP gas with X-COP stars, plus an observed side from another team (3 clusters). Most row-O clusters (18 of 24) have no gas source found.
- CHEX-MATE would add profile gas for 19 pairable clusters once its density profiles are published.

## Gaps

1. **Row O has no gas for 18 of 24 clusters.** These are the low-redshift CIRS (C20d) and CAIRNS (C21a) clusters, and A2199 (C21c). They are not in X-COP, not matched to CHEX-MATE (whose Tier 1 is 0.05 < z < 0.2, per the sample paper), and neither the CIRS nor the CAIRNS observed-side paper names a gas source. A search for gas profiles of these named clusters (the A9·hot-gas leads in the search log, and others) has not been done.
2. **LoCuSS gas is aperture-only.** Martino et al. 2014 tabulates gas masses at three hydrostatic radii, and its Fig. 2 shows profiles for three example clusters only. Mulroy et al. 2019 gives one gas mass within r500,WL. Neither tabulates per-cluster density or density-model parameters. Whether LoCuSS density profiles exist elsewhere (for example in a LoCuSS data release) was not searched.
3. **CHEX-MATE density profiles are not published** (see Summary). There are 19 positional or name matches. The 17 positional matches depend on paper positions (BCG or X-ray), because no name resolver was reachable.
4. **Not read:** Nagarajan et al. 2018 (APEX-SZ gas for 10 clusters); Akamatsu et al. 2011 (A2142); the XXL-100-GC list (Pacaud et al. 2016), so XXL gas coverage rests on the sample statements of Ziparo 2016 and Eckert 2016; the per-cluster tabulation in Mirakhor & Walker 2020 (whether the profile is tabulated was not checked); CHeCS per-cluster density parameters (A9 reader B saw only priors in its Table 2).
5. **Centres not found** for CLASH-X (C3a), Pizzardo 2021 (C20d), Pizzuti 2025 (C29b), Lin et al. 2003 (S2b), Mulroy 2014 (L8), Muzzin 2007 (S16b) and Chiu 2018 (S10b), in the text searched. Girardi 2000 (L2) uses the centres of G98, which was not read.
6. **Component and alias matches (flagged):** A115 ↔ Martino "ABELL 0115s" (position 0.01′; Mulroy 2014 prints "ABELL0115a"); MS 1455.0+2232 ↔ ZwCl 1454.8+2233 (position 0.03′, as in v3).
7. **Stars outside v3:** the A9 records C29a (eRASS1) and C23c (Coma, DESI) carry their own stellar estimates, and C21b (CAIRNS II) carries K-band light profiles. None is a counting star record in coverage v3, so none counts here. Adding them would raise the coherence of Zw1215p0400 (eRASS1) and of Coma, and possibly of the CAIRNS clusters (C21a + C21b), to 2 or 3 inputs.
