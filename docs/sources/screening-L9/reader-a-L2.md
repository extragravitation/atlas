## Questions for the maintainer

- Q1 (L16): D-071 says "printed in a table or released file". Does a single total printed only in running text (Sec. 6) count, as tier 2 or lower? Note that its aperture is the survey area, not a physical radius.
- Q2 (L22a + L22b): The members-only brightness (L22b Table 2, BCG+ICL excluded, no interloper correction, field-of-view aperture) and the BCG+ICL brightness (L22a Table 3) are each printed per cluster. Is adding two printed per-cluster terms allowed, or is that a "reader rebuild"? Does the lack of a field-galaxy correction exclude Msat on its own?
- Q3 (L23b): The paper has an erratum (ApJ 611, 1208, 2004; noted in the arXiv comments). It needs a read to see whether Table 2 or the aperture changed. Otherwise the record meets E: I band, BCG + background-subtracted members + diffuse bound, 500 h⁻¹ kpc projected, printed table.
- Q4 (L24a, L24b): The total cluster light is used only as the denominator of the printed ICL fractions (and, for L24b, next to printed L_ICL and L_BCG+ICL). Dividing a printed luminosity by a printed fraction recovers the total. Is that a forbidden reader rebuild?
- Q5 (L24d): records.md lists A85/A2457/A2670; the paper at this bibcode covers A85, A2457 and IIZw108. Please correct the record label.
- Q6 (L28a, L28b): Neither ChA&A full text was reachable. Can the maintainer supply a copy or another route?
- Q7 (LV3): The VizieR ReadMe could not be read. Please confirm that the catalogue carries per-cluster g/r/i luminosities, and whether the BCG is explicitly in the sum. The text implies but does not state it.
- Q8 (LV4): Do FoF-membership luminosities with a visibility-window weighting, and no fixed aperture, satisfy the "stated aperture" rule?
- Q9 (LV5a, LV5b): Do summed luminosities of bright members only (above a fixed absolute-magnitude cut, no faint-end correction) satisfy D-071? For LV5b, the value is in units of an evolving L*(z), not L☉. Counts below z = 0.30 need the data tables, which the brief forbids downloading.
- Q10 (LV7a): Is an uncorrected summed observed magnitude of flux-limited FoF members a clear X? I applied the eligibility rule (no completeness correction, no aperture).
- Q11 (L18): Which bibcode is paper I and which is paper II? I mapped 550ag829 to paper I and 550g1017 to paper II without ADS confirmation. Both rows are X either way.
- Q12: The brief's specific checks for S4a, S4b and S9b do not apply to this batch; none of those records is in records.md. No VizieR ReadMe was reachable from this session, so every VizieR column claim above comes from the parent paper's printed table notes.
