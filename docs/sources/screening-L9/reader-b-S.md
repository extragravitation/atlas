## Questions for the maintainer

- **Q1 (S3a, S10a):** Under D-071, does a printed magnitude count as a "printed luminosity"? S3a prints rest-frame absolute magnitudes (with extinction and k-corrections in a separate column). S10a prints observed-frame apparent magnitudes, which need a distance modulus and a k-correction applied by the atlas.
- **Q2 (S4b, S4d, S6):** Is BCG coverage acceptable when it is implied but not stated? In S6 it is implied by a BCG/total ratio. In S4b and S4d the total is an integral of a fitted LF, and the text never says whether the BCG is in the fit.
- **Q3 (S1a):** Does a model "effective luminosity" at a fitted effective radius (Table A.3) meet "within a stated aperture"? Totals within r200 appear only in figures.
- **Q4 (S2a):** The per-cluster totals (L200, BCG + members, K_s, 93 clusters) are attributed to Lin, Mohr & Stanford 2004 (paper II), which is not in this list. Should it be added as a record?
- **Q5 (S3a vs S3b):** 12 systems overlap. S3b changes the solar normalisation and uses an X-ray r500 instead of a σ-based r500. Which record governs for the overlapping clusters?
- **Q6 (S4d):** The A2667 luminosity is taken from the literature in K band, while the others are measured in i′. The geometry is also unclear (projected counts normalised to a spherical volume). Should the whole record wait on a ruling, or should A2667 alone be excluded?
- **Q7 (S12b):** arXiv:2007.01084 is "Iron in X-COP" (A&A 646, A92, 2021). Please confirm this is the intended "X-COP stellar masses" record. It is X either way.
- **Q8 (S1b, S13d):** I could not identify 2026yCat..75211292P or 2021ApJ...915..106C, because ADS and the CDS ReadMe were not reachable from here. Please supply titles or arXiv ids.
- **Q9 (process):** For X rows I gave counts and locations, not every cluster name. Please say if full name/z lists are wanted for excluded records too.
