# Patterns: plan (D-085)

**Adopted:** 2026-11-02 (D-085). A Patterns section describes observed regularities in the acceleration discrepancy: where each holds, where it fails, and how well it is measured. It states no cause. Explanations belong on the account pages (Tests), presented from proponents' published claims on all sides.

## The five patterns, in build order

| # | Pattern | What it relates | Data in hand | Reproduction anchor (to be read at source before any criterion is frozen) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Radial acceleration relation (RAR) | g_obs against g_bar, point by point | All six shipped datasets; SPARC's authors' selection already reproduced (2 690 of 2 694 points) | Lelli, McGaugh, Schombert & Pawlowski 2017, ApJ 836, 152 |
| P2 | Baryonic Tully–Fisher relation (BTFR) | Total baryonic mass against flat rotation speed | SPARC Table 1 (V_flat, L[3.6], M_HI) | The published SPARC BTFR (candidate: Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2016, ApJL; to verify) |

| P4 | Central density relation | Central baryonic surface density against central dynamical surface density | SPARC central surface brightness, gas profiles and inner rotation curves | Candidate: Lelli et al. 2016, ApJL; to verify at source, including the dynamical estimator used |

## Rules for every pattern page (neutrality)

1. **Describe, do not fit.** No fitted relation is drawn by default. A published empirical fit may be shown only as a labelled overlay, credited to its source. If one group's fit is offered, other groups' published fits of the same relation are offered on equal terms.
2. **Holds and fails, equally prominent.** Every page shows where the regularity holds, where it fails or is untested, and its measured scatter with the error scales of D-083. Data the pattern cannot use are shown as excluded, with the reason, never hidden.
3. **Reproduce before extending.** Each page first reproduces the authors' published version under a frozen test, as the SPARC selection view does. Only then does it extend to other datasets.
4. **Criteria frozen first.** Definitions are frozen by hash before the data are plotted for the page: what counts as flat, how baryonic mass is summed, central quantities, feature criteria, and which systems are included.
5. **Interpretation lives elsewhere.** Pattern pages link to the account pages and carry no verdict. Words follow the two vocabularies of the project instructions.

## Prerequisites ("clean-up first")

1. **D-083 phase 1 on the site**: point-scale error bars, the whole-system shift view, and the uncertainty terms on dataset and system pages. P1 depends on it, because its scatter must be shown with the error scales.
2. **A pattern page template** in both language layers. It has these sections:
   - definition and source;
   - reproduction result;
   - the figure;
   - where it holds;
   - where it fails or is untested;
   - excluded systems and reasons;
   - published fits (optional overlays);
   - links to accounts;
   - how to cite.
3. **Reading at source** for each anchor paper (P2–P5), recorded with locations before any criterion is written.
4. **Reviewers.** When drafts exist, the authors of each anchor paper are invited to review its reproduction. At least one group working on galaxy-formation explanations of the same relations is invited to review the same pages. Whom to invite is JvH's decision.

## Not in scope here

- New datasets: under the freeze (D-080). Every pattern above uses SPARC, or data already shipped.
- Accounts and their predictions: the Tests section, under its own rules.
