## Method

The frozen Atlas taxonomy is TAXONOMY_v2: 11 kinds (A0–A10), 6 tracers, and 4 forms. I compared it against four field reviews, two from each side:

| Review | Side | Depth read |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Famaey & McGaugh 2012 (Living Review) | MOND | contents + first 100k characters |
| Banik & Zhao 2022 | MOND | roadmap + sections 1–5.6 |
| Bullock & Boylan-Kolchin 2017 | ΛCDM small-scale | contents and abstract |
| Salucci 2019 | dark matter in galaxies, observational | contents |

I also checked about 50 named test systems from these reviews against the Atlas's plotted systems.

**Limits:**
- Banik & Zhao §6–12 (groups, clusters, cosmology) were read at roadmap level only.
- The two dark-matter reviews were read at contents level only.
- A pass of the body text would be needed to say "exhaustive".

## Finding 1. Gaps inside the Atlas's own measurement (g_obs vs g_bar), missing from the frozen list

These are the ones that matter most for an incompleteness charge. All of them measure the pull, but the taxonomy has no row or column for them.

| Missing | What it is | Raised in |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Vertical dynamics** (tracer) | the pull *perpendicular* to a disc, from star motions above and below it; the "Oort discrepancy"; the DiskMass survey | all four reviews (Salucci §4 "Vertical motions"; B&Z §3.1.2; F&M §2) |
| **The Milky Way as a multi-tracer system** | rotation curve, vertical pull, **escape-velocity curve**, streams; data no other galaxy has | F&M §6.5.2, B&Z, Salucci §8 ("The Galaxy") |
| **Tidal streams** (tracer) | streams map the shape of the potential (Pal 5, Sagittarius, GD-1) | F&M §4.2, B&Z §5.2 |
| **Shell galaxies** (tracer / kind) | shell radii trace the potential (NGC 474, NGC 3923); Bílek's own speciality | B&Z §5.4 |
| **Binary galaxies / satellite kinematics** (kind) | relative velocities of galaxy pairs, satellites around isolated hosts | B&Z ("binary galaxy relative velocities"); the survey marked satellite *profiles* ✕, but the **kind** is not listed |
| **The Local Group** (kind / scale) | Milky Way–M31 timing argument, local Hubble flow | F&M §2, B&Z |
| **Direct acceleration measurements** (tracer) | Solar System barycentre acceleration (Gaia); pulsar timing [NR this session, from memory] | B&Z §11.4 |
| **Tidal radii and tidal stability** (observable) | the size at which satellites are stripped: a pull measurement at their edge | B&Z §5.1 |

**Covered by the frozen list but with no data yet:**
- ultra-diffuse galaxies (A4/A7);
- tidal dwarfs and polar rings (A7);
- wide binaries (A1);
- open clusters (A2);
- compact groups (A8);
- colliding clusters (A10);
- strong-lensing time delays.

**These are already listed**, so they are data gaps, not taxonomy gaps.

## Finding 2. Named test systems the reviews use, and whether the Atlas has them

**In:**

- Fornax and Tucana (dwarfs);

**Not in (as of v0.2):**

| Group | Systems |
|:--|:--|

| Famous single galaxies | NGC 1560, AGC 114905, Holmberg II, M94, M101, NGC 1042, NGC 3351 |
| Dwarfs | **Crater II, Antlia II, Carina** (excluded by rule, AU-01), Perseus I, Cetus |

| Early-types | NGC 720, NGC 1521 (the isolated X-ray ellipticals), NGC 1399, 4472, 4486 (M87), 4649, 1407, 5128 (Cen A) |

**The bolded systems are the field's standard test cases.** A reader who looks for them and doesn't find them, with no stated reason, is the incompleteness charge in its most concrete form.

## Finding 3. Evidence that is not g_obs vs g_bar (outside the charter by design)

All four reviews, from both sides, weigh these. The Atlas excludes them by charter. Grouped by origin:

| Origin | Evidence |
|:--|:--|
| Mostly MOND-side reviews | bar fraction, strength and pattern speed; spiral-arm number; survival of thin and bulgeless discs; Freeman limit and LSB number counts; warps; dynamical friction (e.g. Fornax's globular clusters); shell generations; formation of tidal dwarfs; satellite planes; KBC void / Hubble tension; Ly-α absorbers |
| Mostly dark-matter-side reviews | missing satellites (counts); too-big-to-fail; cusp vs core (*partly inside the charter*: inner rotation-curve shape is in the profiles); diversity of rotation curves; starless dark halos; indirect detection |
| Both | Bullet / colliding-cluster offsets (A10 covers the maps); CMB, BBN, BAO, supernovae, large-scale structure, cluster abundance; high-z galaxies and clusters (documented gap); microlensing / MACHOs |

**The neutrality point.** The excluded list is **not symmetric**:
- **The MOND-side checklist** is mostly about structure and dynamics: bars, discs, planes. Excluding these removes many of MOND's claimed successes.
- **The dark-matter side's strongest evidence** (CMB, large-scale structure, Bullet) is also excluded.

**The exclusion cuts both ways, which is defensible. But only if the boundary is stated, with the list of what lies outside it.** Silence about either side reads as selection.

## What this means

1. **The frozen taxonomy is close for kinds, but misses at least four measurement routes for the pull** (vertical dynamics, streams, shells, direct accelerations) and **two kinds** (binary galaxies/satellites, the Local Group). It also has **no explicit home for the Milky Way**.
2. **The largest visible gap is named systems,** not kinds: M33, the LMC, DF2/DF4, Crater II, Carina, Bullet, Pal 5. A "famous test cases" checklist, with each one's status and reason, would answer the incompleteness charge directly.
3. **The by-design exclusions need to be listed, not just implied,** and the list must cover both sides' evidence. That is a page, not new data.

**Not done here (per the instruction):** no taxonomy v3 draft. Before one is drafted, a body-text pass of Banik & Zhao §6–12 and the two dark-matter reviews would make the lists exhaustive rather than contents-level.
